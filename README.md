# Your Favourite Pokémon

Find your *real* favourite Pokémon through 1v1 tournament battles. Pick a side, watch the bracket fill up, crown your champion.

🌐 **Live:** [pokefavourite.vercel.app](https://pokefavourite.vercel.app)

## Game Modes

- **Overall** — full gen bracket, every Pokémon in that generation
- **By Type** — filter by one of 17 types and battle within that subset
- **Challenges** — cross-gen themed tournaments:
  - *Starters* — all first partners from Gen I to V (15 Pokémon)
  - *Legendaries* — 48 legendary and mythical Pokémon across all gens
  - *Eevee Evolutions* — Vaporeon through Glaceon (7 Pokémon)
  - *Pseudo-Legendaries* — the 600-stat club, Dragonite to Hydreigon (6 Pokémon)
  - **★ World Championship** — all 649 Pokémon, one bracket, one true favourite

---

## How It Works

Brackets use **head-to-head elimination**. Small pools (≤10) use linear mode; larger pools use a full bracket with rounds, quarterfinals, semifinals, and a final.

When you reach the final 6, they become **your team** — shown on a team reveal screen before the ace battle that crowns your champion.

You can **undo your last pick** at any point during a battle.

---

## Account & Saves

- Create an account to save picks across devices and contribute to public leaderboards
- **Guest mode** available — plays anonymously via Supabase, votes still count in leaderboards
- Guest picks migrate automatically when you register or log in
- Sessions are autosaved every 10 picks and can be resumed at any time
- **Data export** — download your profile, favourites, and sessions as JSON from Account settings
- Account deletion removes all your data from the database

---

## Sharing

The result screen lets you **share your champion as a story image** (1080×1920 PNG) — includes the Pokémon sprite, your 6-Pokémon team, and the site URL. On mobile, uses the native share sheet; on desktop, downloads directly.
