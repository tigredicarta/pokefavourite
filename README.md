# Your Favourite Pokémon

Find your *real* favourite Pokémon through 1v1 tournament battles. Pick a side, watch the bracket fill up, crown your champion.

Multiple game modes: full-roster brawls, type-only battles, themed cross-roster challenges (Starters, Legendaries, Eevee evolutions, Pseudo-Legendaries), and the **World Championship** — every Pokémon, one bracket, one true favourite.

Save your picks to your account, resume sessions across devices, share your champion as a story image, and see how your taste compares to the community on the public leaderboards.

🎮 Pixel-perfect retro style — Press Start 2P, CRT scanlines, faithful sprite work.

🌐 **Live:** [pokefavourite.vercel.app](https://pokefavourite.vercel.app)

🔒 Privacy-first — no tracking, no ads, no third-party analytics. Your data stays yours.
